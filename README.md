## ✨ Overview

**FitPulse** is a fitness and workout tracking app that gives users personalized training plans, real-time progress tracking, and the motivation to stay consistent — whether they're training at home, in the gym, or with a coach.

---

## 💼 Why Users (and Clients) Love This

- 🏋️ **Personalized Workout Plans** — Routines tailored to goals, fitness level, and available equipment
- 📈 **Real-Time Progress Tracking** — Visual charts for weight, reps, strength, and body measurements
- ⌚ **Wearable Integration** — Syncs with smartwatches for heart rate, steps, and calories burned
- 🎥 **Guided Video Workouts** — Step-by-step exercise demos to train with correct form
- 🔥 **Streaks & Achievements** — Gamified milestones that keep users motivated and consistent
- 🧑‍🏫 **Coach & Trainer Mode** — Trainers can assign plans and monitor client progress remotely
- 🍎 **Nutrition & Calorie Tracking** — Simple food logging paired with workout data
- 👥 **Community Challenges** — Group challenges and leaderboards for friendly competition
- 🔔 **Smart Reminders** — Workout and hydration reminders that adapt to the user's routine
- 📊 **Progress Reports** — Weekly and monthly summaries showing real, measurable results

---

## 🧱 How It Works

| Feature | Experience |
|---|---|
| **Set a Goal** | Users choose a goal — strength, weight loss, endurance — and get a custom plan |
| **Train With Guidance** | Video-guided workouts keep form correct and motivation high |
| **Track Everything** | Reps, sets, weight, and vitals are logged automatically or manually |
| **Stay Accountable** | Streaks, reminders, and community challenges keep users consistent |
| **See Real Progress** | Visual reports show measurable improvement over time |

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (Under the Hood)

**Mobile App:** React Native (single codebase for iOS & Android)
**Backend:** Node.js, TypeScript for workout logic, progress data, and coaching features
**Integrations:** Wearable & smartwatch data sync for real-time health metrics
**Cloud Infrastructure:** AWS for secure, scalable, always-available hosting

This stack keeps the experience **smooth and motivating**, whether tracking one workout or a full training history.
